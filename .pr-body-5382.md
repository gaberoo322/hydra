## Summary

dispatch-intent 2/5. A `qa_orch` dispatch now carries a typed Dispatch Intent: `ReviewLane(anchor, rest)` (the selector's parked-filtered lane head plus the parked-filtered remainder, in order) or `ReviewSelfSelect()` (degraded board read, the #3709 fail-open arm). The renderer's qa_orch branch is a pure function of the intent; its Turn Snapshot read of the needs-qa lane is deleted, so a parked issue can never reach a QA prompt through the renderer (#5319 bug class). The selector's pin-only-when-head-changed distinction is removed: it always names the head it chose. The head-and-rest-of-lane intent also closes the latent hole where the unpinned arm let the renderer print a parked tail as "the rest of the lane".

Rendered-text before/after (only these hunks change): the pinned and lane-head phrasings unify into the ReviewLane sentence; the pinned shape regains its rest-of-lane list; the move-on clause is bounded to the intent ("next issue in the lane above (never one outside it)"). ReviewSelfSelect renders today's unpinned text verbatim.
